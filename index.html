How do I get my Instagram export?
  1. In Instagram, go to Settings → Accounts Center → Your information and permissions → Download your information.
  2. Choose "Some of your information" and select only Followers and following.
  3. Set the format to JSON (not HTML) and submit the request.
  4. Instagram will notify you when the export is ready to download (usually minutes to a few hours).
  5. Download the ZIP and drop it here — or unzip it and select the files from connections/followers_and_following/.

Your data never leaves this browser tab.
